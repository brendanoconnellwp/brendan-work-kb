# Corey Ganim Method — Synthesis & Internalization Guide

## What Makes His Method Work

Corey built a repeatable sales engine. The pattern isn't hype — it's a **pipeline**:

```
Free Value → Audit ($999) → Concierge ($1K/hr/$8K MRR) → Course ($799/yr)
```

Each step feeds the next. The audit creates trust AND a natural upsell path. The content gives away the playbook, which builds authority AND qualifies leads.

---

## The 4-Part System He Actually Runs

### 1. The Entry Offer: AI Audit ($999)
**Why it works:** It's the easiest "yes" a business owner will give you. Low price, no long-term commitment, clear deliverable.

**His pitch:** *"I'll audit your workflows and show you the 3-7 places AI could save time, recover revenue, or reduce bottlenecks."*

**The 9-slide deliverable:**
- Title slide
- Current state assessment (find the leaks)
- Effort vs Impact matrix (what's easy to fix vs what's valuable)
- Tool stack recommendation
- 4-day quick wins plan
- Financial ROI projection (monthly, black and white)
- Implementation roadmap
- Upsell/next steps
- Template link (he gives it away)

**The framing:** Not "I'll build you AI." He finds where the process is broken FIRST, then applies AI. This is critical positioning.

### 2. The Audit Process
Before automating anything:
1. **Watch the workflow happen** — Where does the request come in? Who touches it? What gets copied/pasted? Where does it wait?
2. **Identify the leaks** — Leads not answered, quotes slow, same questions repeated, no follow-up, owner doing admin

**The ordering framework:**
1. **Audit** — Find the broken parts
2. **Optimize** — Fix the process
3. **Automate** — THEN add AI

*"Broken process + AI = faster mess"*

### 3. The Upsell: AI Concierge ($1K/hr → $8K MRR)
After the audit, clients are already bought in on the diagnosis. The upsell is natural: "Now let me build the solution."

The "Concierge" framing is smart — it sounds high-touch, personalized, and removes the fear of dealing with "AI tech."

### 4. The Content Engine
**Strategy:** *"Give a ridiculous amount of value for free, no strings attached"*

He gives clients his entire playbook. He gives away the exact template. He responds to every sensible DM. This builds:
- Trust (he's not hiding anything)
- Authority (he clearly knows what he's doing)
- Inbound (people DM him asking to buy)

## The Specific Language You Should Steal

These aren't hooks — they're **positioning frameworks**:

| His Language | What It Does |
|---|---|
| "Find the leak" | Changes from "sell AI" to "diagnose problem" |
| "Effort vs Impact" | Gives a decision framework, not a pitch |
| "Speed to lead" | Frames the problem around money lost, not tech missing |
| "Broken process + AI = faster mess" | Pre-sells the audit before the automation |
| "3-7 places AI could save time, recover revenue, or reduce bottlenecks" | Makes the offer concrete and scoped |
| "Personal AGI in 12 hours" | Makes the impossible feel achievable |

### Every Morning (5 min)
Read ONE of the 9 transcripts in `sales-playbook/`. Pick up one new phrase or framework. Write it down.

### Every Client Call
Before the call, ask yourself:
- **"Where's the leak?"** — What's actually broken in their workflow?
- **"Effort vs Impact?"** — What's the highest-value, lowest-effort fix?
- **"Speed to lead?"** — How do leads come in and who sees them?

### Every Piece of Content You Make
Use his 3-part structure:
1. **Audit** — Describe the problem/broken process
2. **Optimize** — Show the fix (no AI needed yet)
3. **Automate** — THEN bring in the AI solution

## How to Apply This to Your Clients (Article & Digital Anchor)

### For Article (enterprise clients)
The audit model works at ANY scale. For a large org, the $999 price becomes "free assessment included with engagement." But the **framing** is the same:
- Find the leak in their workflow first
- Show the effort vs impact matrix
- Quote the financial ROI in black and white
- THEN propose the solution

### For Digital Anchor (SMB clients)
Directly clone his model:
1. **Free blog post/video** that walks through "How to find AI opportunities in your business"
2. **$X audit** (match his $999 or whatever fits your market)
3. **Implementation** as the upsell
4. **Content recycling** — every audit becomes a case study becomes a post

### For Your Own Content
Use his titles as a template and swap your niche:

| His Title | → Your Version |
|---|---|
| "I charge $999 for an AI audit" | "I charge $X for a [niche] workflow audit" |
| "Broken process + AI = faster mess" | Same — universal truth |
| "How to sell AI to boomers who hate AI" | "How to sell [your service] to [your audience]" |
| "The $1K/hr business model nobody's talking about" | "The $X/hr [your niche] model nobody's talking about" |
| "5-step framework for AI services" | "5-step framework for [your service]" |
