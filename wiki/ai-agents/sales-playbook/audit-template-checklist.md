# The $999 AI Audit — 9-Slide Deliverable Checklist

> Adapted from Corey Ganim's AI Tools Assessment template. His deliverable is built in Claude Design (formerly Gamma). This is the framework he charges $999 for and then gives away for free.

## Before the Audit — Discovery Call (45 min)

**Goal:** Gather the raw material to fill in the template. Record the call — you'll drop the transcript into Claude to auto-fill.

### Questions to Ask
- [ ] Where does the lead/customer request first come in? (form, phone, email, DM, booking page)
- [ ] Who sees it first? What happens then?
- [ ] What gets copied/pasted manually between systems?
- [ ] Where does the process wait/bottleneck?
- [ ] What breaks when the usual person is out?
- [ ] How much time per week does the owner spend on admin?
- [ ] What's the most common question customers ask repeatedly?
- [ ] What task would they pay someone $20/hr to do?
- [ ] What's their monthly spend on tools they don't fully use?
- [ ] What's the #1 thing that would save them 5+ hours/week?

## The 9-Slide Template

### Slide 1: Title Slide
- [ ] Client name
- [ ] "AI Tools Assessment"
- [ ] Assessment date
- [ ] Business type / industry
- [ ] Color-accented, clean design (match their brand)

### Slide 2: Executive Summary
- [ ] **Primary pain point** — the #1 thing you identified
- [ ] **Primary outcome** — what they'll get from acting
- [ ] **Primary ROI lever** chosen: *Effectiveness* (makes money), *Efficiency* (saves time), or *Quality* (improves output)

### Slide 3: Current State Assessment — "The Leaks"
List what's broken. Be specific:
- [ ] Lead response gaps (how long until someone answers?)
- [ ] Quote/proposal delays
- [ ] Repetitive manual work (copy/paste, data entry)
- [ ] Customer questions answered repeatedly
- [ ] Sales follow-ups that fall through
- [ ] Owner doing admin work
- [ ] Missing information causing delays
- [ ] Tools not integrated

### Slide 4: Effort vs Impact Matrix
A 2x2 grid:
```
               Low Effort          High Effort
High Impact    [QUICK WINS]        [STRATEGIC PROJECTS]
Low Impact     [LOW HANGING FRUIT] [AVOID / DE-prioritize]
```
- [ ] Place each opportunity in the right quadrant
- [ ] Highlight 3-5 quick wins (low effort, high impact)

### Slide 5: Recommended Tool Stack
- [ ] For each pain point: the specific tool/approach
- [ ] Show what you'd replace/eliminate (cost savings)
- [ ] Example: "Replace manual QA with Claude → saves 8 hrs/week"

### Slide 6: 4-Day Quick Wins Plan
A concrete, immediate plan:
- [ ] **Day 1:** Setup — connect the tools, configure
- [ ] **Day 2:** Automate the first leak (highest-impact quick win)
- [ ] **Day 3:** Automate the second leak
- [ ] **Day 4:** Review, adjust, and handoff

*"If it doesn't work by Day 4, they didn't even risk a real engagement."*

### Slide 7: Financial Impact (Monthly ROI)
Show it in black and white:
- [ ] Hours saved per week (e.g., "15 hrs/week → $X/month")
- [ ] Revenue recovered (e.g., "leads not lost → $X/month")
- [ ] Total monthly ROI projection
- [ ] Cost of proposed solution
- [ ] Net monthly savings/profit

### Slide 8: Implementation Roadmap
- [ ] Phase 1: Immediate quick wins (Week 1)
- [ ] Phase 2: Process optimization (Week 2-3)
- [ ] Phase 3: Automation build (Week 3-4)
- [ ] Phase 4: Monitoring & iteration (Ongoing)

### Slide 9: Next Steps + Upsell
- [ ] What the audit delivered (recap value)
- [ ] Option A: DIY with the template (they can use the template themselves)
- [ ] Option B: "Let me build this for you" (AI Concierge / implementation upsell)
- [ ] Pricing for implementation
- [ ] Call to action (schedule the build call)

## The Upsell Script

After presenting the audit:

> *"We found 7 places AI can save you time or make you money. The 4-day quick wins plan covers 3 of them that you can start immediately with what you've got here. But if you want me to build out the full system — the automated lead response, the quote generator, the customer FAQ agent — I can have that running in 2 weeks. My build rate is $1,000/month retainer or $X/project. Want me to put together a proposal?"*

## Key Principles

1. **Every report pulls ONE of three ROI levers**: Effectiveness (makes money), Efficiency (saves time), Quality (improves product)
2. **Show financials in black and white** — "This saves you 15 hrs/week at $50/hr = $3,000/month"
3. **Give away the template** — builds trust, creates referral loops
4. **The audit IS the sales call** — after slide 9, the upsell is natural
5. **Broken process + AI = faster mess** — always audit and optimize before adding AI
