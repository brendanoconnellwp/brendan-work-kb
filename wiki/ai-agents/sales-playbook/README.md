### 2. The Process Order (Anti-Failure)
1. **Audit** — Watch the workflow. Where does the request come in? Who touches it? What gets copied/pasted? Where does it wait?
2. **Optimize** — Fix the broken process first
3. **Automate** — Only then add AI ("Broken process + AI = faster mess")

### 3. Speed to Lead Offer Framework
- Where does the lead arrive? (form/phone/email/DM/booking/portal)
- Who sees it first? ("Whoever checks the inbox" = problem)

### 4. AI Audit Sales Pitch
> "I'll audit your workflows and show you the 3-7 places AI could save time, recover revenue, or reduce bottlenecks."

### 5. The $1,000/hr Business Model
- Achieve "personal AGI" in 12 hours → sell as service for $5K
- Three ROI levers: Effectiveness (makes money), Efficiency (saves time), ...
